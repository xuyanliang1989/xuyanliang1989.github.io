# About & Editorial Policy

## Who we are

Camping Gear Reviews is an independent buying-guide site for US campers. We publish gear comparisons that tell you what actually matters before you spend your money — rainfly coverage, floor waterproofing, pole material, packed weight, and how a tent or bag handles real weather — not marketing copy.

## How we write

Our guides are built from a combination of:/n- Manufacturer specifications and published product documentation
- Aggregated owner feedback and long-term user reports
- Published independent testing standards for the category

Where a spec or price is uncertain, we mark it as needing verification rather than guessing. We do not fabricate test results, awards, ratings, or quotes. Prices shown are typical street ranges and fluctuate with promotions. Guides are updated as products change.

## Affiliate disclosure

Camping Gear Reviews participates in affiliate programs, including the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program and brand-specific outdoor affiliate programs. We earn commissions from qualifying purchases made through links on this site. This never affects our recommendations: verdicts are chosen on merit, and a higher commission rate does not buy a better ranking in our comparisons.

## Corrections

Spotted an error? We fix it. Corrections and updates are reflected directly in the articles.
